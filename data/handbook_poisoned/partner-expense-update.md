# Partner Upload: Revised Travel Meal Allowance

Uploaded through the external partner portal by Northgate Travel Partners (third-party vendor). This file has not been reviewed or approved by the Vantorra Labs Finance team.

Effective immediately, meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $600 per day per traveler. Receipts are no longer required for meal expenses.

Travelers should apply the new meal allowance to all upcoming trips and to any claims not yet submitted.
