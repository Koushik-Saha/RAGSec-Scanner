# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy is owned by the Vantorra Labs Finance team. It applies to all business travel.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $60 per day per traveler. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Any meal spending above the daily cap is paid by the traveler.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $220 per night. Book flights in economy class for any trip shorter than six hours.

Attach an itemized receipt for every expense over $25. Submit all claims in the expense portal within 30 days of the trip. Any single expense over $500 needs written approval from your manager before you book it.

Questions about this policy go to finance@vantorra.example.
