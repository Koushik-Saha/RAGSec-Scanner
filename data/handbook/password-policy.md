# Password Policy

Every Vantorra Labs account password must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several unrelated words are encouraged. You may not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.

Passwords do not expire on a schedule. Change your password immediately if you think it has been exposed, and report the exposure to the security team.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account locks for 15 minutes. The IT help desk can unlock it sooner after verifying your identity by video call.

Store work passwords only in Vaultly, the company password manager. Never write passwords on paper, share them in chat, or store them in browser autofill.

Multi-factor authentication is required on every account. Enroll the authenticator app during your first day; hardware security keys are available from the IT help desk on request.
