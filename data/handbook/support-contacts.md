# IT Support Contacts

The IT help desk handles laptops, accounts, VPN, and software requests. Email it-help@vantorra.example or call extension 4357. The help desk is staffed from 8:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday.

Report security incidents, such as a lost laptop, a phishing email, or an exposed password, to security@vantorra.example. The security team monitors this address 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

For urgent outages outside help desk hours, call the on-call line at 555-0142.

Walk-in support is available at the IT desk in room 3B.
