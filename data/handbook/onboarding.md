# New Employee IT Onboarding

Welcome to Vantorra Labs. Complete these IT steps in order during your first day.

Step 1: Collect your laptop from the IT desk in room 3B. Bring photo ID. The laptop arrives with disk encryption already turned on.

Step 2: Sign in and set a new password of at least 14 characters, following the Password Policy.

Step 3: Install Vaultly, the company password manager, and save your new password in it.

Step 4: Enroll the authenticator app for multi-factor authentication.

Step 5: Install the SecureLink VPN client and connect once to vpn.vantorra.example, following the VPN Setup guide.

Step 6: Complete the online security awareness training within 7 days of your start date. Access to the expense portal is enabled only after the training is complete.
